The SeatSpeed Solution

Core Features & Workflow

Passenger-Facing Features

  • Advance Seat Booking:
    • Passengers can reserve their seats digitally ahead of time, ensuring they only travel to the departure ranks once the vehicle is ready.
  • Urgent-Trip Emergency Flag:
    • A dedicated prioritizing signal for passengers travelling to distant clinics or hospitals for critical health appointments.

Driver-Facing Features

  • Real-Time Ride Matching:
    • Automatically connects drivers with registered passengers waiting along their scheduled route.

How Advance Booking Transforms Taxi Travel

SeatSpeed is designed as a hybrid digital platform tailored specifically for minibus taxi operations without requiring expensive hardware or altering traditional taxi association routes.

By shifting from a pure "fill-and-go" model to a pre-allocated matching system, commuters in rural and peri-urban areas gain predictability over their daily transport schedule.

Bridge Between High-Tech and Feature Phones

Recognizing that not all passengers or taxi marshals have high-end smartphones or uncapped mobile data, SeatSpeed incorporates a dual-channel communication structure.Smartphone users interact via an intuitive mobile Web app, while feature phone users connect seamlessly through USSD menus and automated SMS updates.

Important Notice

Please note that this website presents a concept proposal. The platform does not host active logins, user profiles, live tracking databases, or real-time payment gateways. It is created purely as an educational design to show how digital coordination can solve real transport problems in vulnerable communities.
