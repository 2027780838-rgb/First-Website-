Ending Long Waits at the Taxi Rank

Millions of daily commuters lose hours waiting for minibus taxis to fill up before departing. SeatSpeed is a project raising awareness about transit delays and proposing advance booking solutions.

Concept Phase

Why Minibus Taxi Efficiency Matters

Minibus taxis carry over 65% of South Africa's public transport passengers. However, traditional rank operations require taxis to be 100% full before departing, causing unpredictable delays.
