The Taxi-Waiting Problem

The "fill-and-go" system forces taxis to remain parked at ranks until every seat is occupied. For long-distance and rural routes, this can mean waiting up to three hours.

The Current System vs. SeatSpeed

Below is a direct comparison showing how our proposed conceptual framework addresses these everyday public transport bottlenecks.

Rural Transport Challenge Proposed SeatSpeed Concept
Unpredictable Waiting Times
Taxis have no set timetables, forcing rural commuters to wait hours at ranks. 		Advance Seat Booking
Commuters can reserve their seats in advance, ensuring they only travel to the rank when the vehicle is full.
Safety & Physical Stress
Vulnerable and elderly commuters have to hike long distances to search for operating drivers. 		Real-Time Ride Matching
Passengers are matched with registered local drivers, reducing roadside wait times and improving safety.
Dangerous Medical Delays
Indefinite waiting during health emergencies can lead to life-threatening transport delays. 		Urgent-Trip Emergency Flag
A specialized scheduling flag allows passengers with critical medical needs to signal travel urgency.
